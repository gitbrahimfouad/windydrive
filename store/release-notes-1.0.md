## App Store Connect (What's New in This Version)

**English**

First release of Windy Drive!

• One-tap driving: tap to steer against the wind
• An endless road, different every time
• The road gets tougher the farther you go: faster, tighter turns, narrower sections
• Wind from the left or from the right, with a separate record for each
• Day and night modes
• Game Center leaderboards, one per wind direction
• Share your distance as a picture
• Works offline
• English and French

**Français**
