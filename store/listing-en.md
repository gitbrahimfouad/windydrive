**Description** (1630/4000)

The wind never stops. Your car never stops. One tap is all you have.

Windy Drive is a simple, addictive one-tap driving game. A side wind constantly pushes your car towards the edge of the road. Tap the screen to steer the other way, find your rhythm, and keep going as far as you can.

HOW TO PLAY
• The wind pushes your car to one side, all the time.
• Every tap gives a steering kick in the opposite direction.
• Stay on the road. Leave it and the run is over.
• Your score is the distance you drive, in meters.

WHY YOU'LL KEEP COMING BACK
• One-tap controls: learn in seconds, improve for hours.
• An endless road, different every time: bends to the left, bends to the right, long straights and sections that suddenly narrow.
• Fair from the start: the first turns are gentle, then the road keeps you on your toes.
• Instant restart: one tap and you're driving again.
• Chase your best: beat your record, and see how close you got when you miss it.

YOUR WIND, YOUR RULES
Choose whether the wind blows from the left or from the right. Each direction has its own personal record, so you can master both.

BRIGHT AND SMOOTH
• A colourful top-down countryside with red-and-white curbs, trees and hay bales.
• Switch to night mode for a headlight drive after dark.
• Smooth, responsive controls and satisfying crash effects.
• Available in English and French.

SHARE YOUR DISTANCE
Finished a great run? Send your score as a ready-made picture and challenge your friends to do better.

PLAY ANYWHERE
Windy Drive works fully offline. No account, no sign-up: just open the app and drive.

Think you can beat the wind? Tap to start.
